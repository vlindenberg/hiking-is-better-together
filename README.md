# Hiking Is Better Together

Create a visually striking, modern and fully responsive **promotional website / brand landing page** for a social hiking app.

IMPORTANT: This is NOT the actual app. Do not build a functional app dashboard, login system, full messaging platform, search interface or complete app experience. The purpose of this website is to advertise and present the hiking app, explain its concept, communicate its benefits and encourage people to discover it.

Think of this as the official launch website for a new lifestyle and outdoor brand. It should feel like an exciting campaign website rather than a traditional technology product website.

**THE APP CONCEPT**

The app connects people who love hiking but don't always have someone to go with. Users can discover hikes organised by other people, join existing groups, create their own hikes and meet new people through shared outdoor experiences.

The concept is similar to BlaBlaCar, but for hiking.

The target audience is anyone looking for hiking partners, whether they are beginners, experienced hikers, solo travellers or people who simply want to meet new people.

The central message is:
**HIKING IS BETTER TOGETHER.**

The website should communicate freedom, adventure, connection, discovery and community.

**1. VISUAL REFERENCES**

Use these websites as inspiration for the overall visual direction, layout, typography, animations and creative experience:

- https://hyround.italgas.it
- https://noho.ink
- https://www.duofankaar.com

Do not copy them. Take inspiration from their bold art direction, experimental layouts, oversized typography, visual storytelling, unexpected compositions and interactive scrolling experiences.

I am also providing three visual references:
- A Pantone colour palette.
- A Nike Tech Pack typography reference.
- An editorial typography reference featuring oversized serif lettering.

Use these as the main visual identity references.

**2. COLOUR PALETTE — STRICTLY USE THESE COLOURS**

Use only these five colours as the website's visual palette:

- Sun Glare: #D4FF00 — vibrant neon lime.
- Exuberant Orange: #FF5128 — energetic orange.
- Blue Violet: #6B56C8 — expressive purple.
- Cloud Dancer: #F5F2EC — warm off-white.
- Darkest Hour: #1F1D1D — almost-black.

Use Cloud Dancer and Darkest Hour as the main background and text colours. Use lime, orange and purple for graphic elements, highlights, buttons and sections.

Do not introduce additional accent colours or use the typical green-and-brown outdoor aesthetic.

**3. TYPOGRAPHY**

Use two contrasting typographic styles:

- Display font: an elegant, high-contrast editorial serif, inspired by the third reference image. Use it for oversized headlines and expressive statements.
- Secondary font: a bold, clean, modern sans-serif, inspired by the Nike Tech Pack reference. Use it for navigation, smaller text, labels and buttons.

Typography should be a major part of the visual identity. Experiment with huge headlines, different scales, overlapping words and asymmetrical positioning.

**4. WEBSITE STRUCTURE**

Create a single-page promotional website with the following sections:

**SECTION 1 — HERO / FIRST IMPRESSION**

This should be the most visually impressive section.

- A huge headline: "HIKING IS BETTER TOGETHER."
- A short introduction: "Find your people. Discover new trails. Make every hike an adventure."
- Two calls to action: "Discover the app" and "How it works".
- Large, expressive outdoor photography showing people hiking together.
- Bold graphic shapes and colourful visual elements using the brand palette.
- A visually interesting navigation bar with the app logo/name.

The hero should immediately communicate that this is a social hiking platform.

**SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM / THE IDEA**

Introduce the problem the app solves.

Headline idea: "LOVE HIKING. DON'T HAVE A CREW?"

Explain that many people want to go hiking but struggle to find friends or people with similar interests to join them.

Then introduce the solution:
"Meet the app that brings hikers together."

Use engaging typography, short text and visual storytelling rather than long paragraphs.

**SECTION 3 — HOW IT WORKS**

Explain the three main things users can do through the app.

1. **DISCOVER A HIKE**
Explore hikes created by other people and find an adventure that suits you.

2. **JOIN THE CREW**
Join existing hiking groups, meet new people and share the experience.

3. **CREATE YOUR OWN ADVENTURE**
Organise a hike, choose your route and invite others to join you.

Present these as three visually distinct, creative sections or cards. Include illustrative images, graphic elements or app interface previews.

**SECTION 4 — SHOWCASE THE APP**

This section is extremely important.

Show attractive visual mockups of the actual mobile app displayed inside realistic smartphone frames.

Create visual previews of:
- The home screen.
- The Explore / Join a Hike screen.
- The AI hiking assistant.
- The Create a Hike screen.
- The Hike Details screen.
- The Group Chat screen.

These should be promotional visual previews, not functional app interfaces. Their purpose is to give visitors an idea of what the app looks like and what they can do with it.

Use large smartphone mockups, overlapping compositions, interesting angles and colourful graphic backgrounds. Make this section feel like a creative product launch campaign.

**SECTION 5 — AI FEATURE**

Introduce the app's AI hiking assistant.

Headline: "NOT SURE WHERE TO GO? LET AI FIND YOUR NEXT ADVENTURE."

Explain that users can describe the kind of hiking experience they want, such as peaceful nature, beautiful views, a social adventure or somewhere less crowded.

The AI recommends suitable hikes and helps users discover alternative destinations beyond the most popular trails.

Make this section visually distinctive, using the lime and purple palette, expressive typography and an illustrative preview of the AI feature.

**SECTION 6 — THE COMMUNITY**

Focus on the social aspect of the app.

Headline: "COME FOR THE HIKE. STAY FOR THE PEOPLE."

Show photographs of groups hiking, laughing, exploring nature and meeting new people.

Explain that the app is not only about finding trails, but also about creating connections and making outdoor experiences more accessible.

**SECTION 7 — FINAL CALL TO ACTION**

Finish with a bold, memorable campaign-style section.

Headline: "YOUR NEXT ADVENTURE STARTS WITH A HELLO."

Short text: "Find your hiking crew and discover where the trail takes you."

Include a prominent button: "Discover the app".

Make the final section colourful, energetic and visually memorable.

**5. ART DIRECTION AND INTERACTIONS**

The website should feel like an experimental creative campaign designed by a contemporary branding studio.

- Use oversized editorial typography.
- Use large-scale outdoor photography.
- Experiment with asymmetrical grids and unconventional compositions.
- Combine photography with bold graphic shapes and colourful blocks.
- Use playful transitions, smooth scrolling and subtle entrance animations.
- Include interesting hover interactions.
- Use overlapping images and typography where appropriate.
- Avoid generic website templates.
- Avoid standard corporate layouts.
- Avoid making every section look like a collection of rounded cards.
- Maintain visual consistency throughout the website.

The design should feel youthful, bold, playful, expressive, social and adventurous.

**6. IMPORTANT DESIGN CONSTRAINTS**

Remember that this is an ADVERTISEMENT / PROMOTIONAL WEBSITE, not the hiking app itself.

Do not create:
- A functional app dashboard.
- A full search and filtering system.
- A working booking or joining system.
- A functional group chat.
- A complete user profile system.
- A complicated sign-up or login experience.

Instead, use visual mockups, illustrations, screenshots-style previews and short explanations to communicate these features.

The main purpose is to make visitors understand what the app does, why it is useful and why they would want to use it.

**FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION**

I want this website to look more like a bold lifestyle brand campaign than a typical hiking or technology website.

Combine:
- Experimental editorial design.
- Oversized serif and bold sans-serif typography.
- Vibrant Pantone-inspired colours.
- Beautiful outdoor photography.
- Playful graphic compositions.
- Creative product mockups.
- Strong visual storytelling.

Make the website visually impressive from the first screen, with a clear narrative that introduces the problem, presents the app as the solution, showcases its features and ends with an exciting call to action.

Prioritise art direction, storytelling and visual identity over functionality.
